    **CareSignal is a digital health company I co-founded where I led Product and Brand.** It uses automated SMS and IVR  messages to collect health data from patients; its web platform highlights patients who might be candidates for outreach from a provider, usually a nurse. 

    Our design system needed to: 
    - Support our users in quickly pinpointing patients with unaddressed needs
    - Enable our engineering team to iterate quickly with little design oversight
    - Strengthen users’ trust in our company and differentiate our brand
