introduction: |
  When we started, we wanted to build trust in prospects by conveying that we brought the rigor of pharmaceutical research to our digital programs so we named ourselves Epharmix (i.e. “Electronic Pharmaceuticals”). That initial positioning helped us attract our first customers but it became a risk as we grew. The proof? Our Sales team had to spend their elevator pitches explaining why we weren’t directly involved with manufacturing or administering pharmaceuticals.

  I led the rebrand as Creative Director and owned the final visual direction and visualizations. I collaborated closely across departments to derisk the updates associated with our rebranding. Jamie Lawrence (Graphic Designer) was a key collaborator. He shaped direction and explorations, refined details, and offered critique that sharpened the work.
