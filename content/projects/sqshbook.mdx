introduction: I designed a streamlined and joyful submission experience for SQSHBook, a queer-centered, community-owned database of nearly 2,000 resources mostly centered around the Saint Louis metropolitan area. It is a web application maintained by St. Louis Queer Support & Healing (SQSH). The resources and reviews hosted on SQSHBook contribute to the grant opportunities available to SQSH. I also established analytics for SQSHBook and the data has informed product and program decisions-making across the organization.
