introduction: SQSHBook is a queer-centered, community-owned database of nearly 2,000 resources generally located around the Saint Louis metropolitan area. We needed to streamline the existing experience to encourage community submissions. Working with SQSH's data team and testing with community members, I streamlined the form down to five fields.
